# Material comparison

The existing A and B captures and the Hybrid capture were rendered at 1440 × 960 in Chromium and Firefox with the same palette direction and representative gauge values. A and B share the same markup with each other; Hybrid adds model cards and a functional inspector sample, changes the gauge rendering and typography, and therefore is not a controlled pixel-difference comparison. Compare the primary panel visually, not whole-image difference scores.

- **A — Optical Liquid Glass:** brighter instrument and chromatic edge from the package. Its SVG displacement filters a duplicated scene, which is not a faithful refraction of arbitrary live content. Refraction was weak on the smooth scene and its brighter surface competes with readings.
- **B — Frosted Titanium:** calmer neutral material and legible readings, but less separation of the main instrument from ordinary cards.
- **Hybrid:** carries B's darker structure and secondary surfaces, with a brighter upper rim and restrained internal reflection on the instrument and Details sample. The screenshots show a distinct surface hierarchy and readable gauge values. The treatment remains a visual approximation rather than measured refraction. In light mode the material is more subtle and could benefit from closer review on the actual display.

The sample has not received user approval, comprehensive accessibility review, or target-hardware performance validation. It is a candidate for review rather than a production-ready reference.

## Final refinement

The final candidate keeps Hybrid's gauge geometry, cards and palette. It strengthens the upper optical highlight and makes mobile Details more opaque, while adding a visible navigation overflow cue. The final captures demonstrate the revised presentation, not new physical refraction. User approval and the pending reference gate are still required.
